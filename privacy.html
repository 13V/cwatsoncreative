Privacy

This page explains what happens to the details you share through this website.

What I collect

When you send an enquiry, I receive the details you type into the form: your name, email, and anything else you choose to include, such as a phone number, date or message.

How I use it

Only to reply to your enquiry and, if you book, to plan your session. I don’t sell or share your details for marketing.

Photos

Some session photos appear on this website and on Instagram. If you’d like a photo of you removed, just ask and I’ll take it down.

Questions or changes

To see, correct or delete the details you’ve sent, message me on Instagram at @cwatson.creative.
